# Class B Camper Van Market Census, North America
Compiled Oct 14, 2026 from manufacturer lineup pages and RV industry coverage.
PLANNING DOCUMENT, not publishable facts. Every model enters the site only after fact-ledger verification.

## Factory manufacturers (current models)
- **Winnebago:** Revel, Revel Sport, Travato, Solis, Solis Pocket (current). Roam, Era, Boldt: DISCONTINUED (Boldt last brochure 2023; the old quiz recommended it).
- **Thor Motor Coach:** Scope, Rize, Sequence, Tellaro (ProMaster); Palladium, Talavera (Transit AWD, new); Sanctuary, Tranquility (Sprinter AWD). Dazzle discontinued for 2026.
- **Coachmen:** Beyond (Transit AWD), Galleria, Nova, Pixel (new). Cross Trail is a Class C, excluded.
- **Airstream:** Interstate 19 / 19X, Interstate 24GL / 24GT / 24GLX, Rangeline (ProMaster). Atlas is B+, flagged.
- **Jayco:** Swift, Terrain (Sprinter AWD), Comet. Granite Ridge flagged B+/C.
- **Entegra Coach:** Ethos, Launch (Sprinter AWD, sister to Jayco Terrain), Expanse (Transit AWD).
- **Forest River (own brand):** Lucent (Transit) and Sommar (ProMaster), both brand-new for 2027.
- **Tiffin:** GH1, GH2 (Sprinter AWD). Cahaba discontinued.
- **Pleasure-Way:** Plateau, Ascent, Ontour 2.0 / 2.2, Lexor. Tofino and Rekon DISCONTINUED (not on current lineup page).
- **Grech RV:** Strada-ion, Terreno-ion, Turismo-ion, Vacanza-ion (all Sprinter, lithium line).
- **Midwest Automotive Designs:** Passage, Patriot / Patriot Cruiser.
- **Chinook RV:** Bayside is their only remaining pure Class B (Summit/Maverick reclassified B+ for 2027).
- **Roadtrek:** Zion line, Play line, Chase, SS Agile, Westfalia Wave line. Still selling new Class B in 2026/2027 per dealer inventory; manufacturer page confirmation pending.

Factory side alone: roughly 45 current models.

## Custom / upfitter builders (current)
- **Storyteller Overland:** MODE OG, MODE XO (full bath), MODE XL CREW, MODE XL TOUR (Sprinter).
- **Sportsmobile:** custom builds on Sprinter / Transit / ProMaster / Chevy Express; no fixed models.
- **Outside Van:** Approach, Syncline, Baseline, plus custom (Portland OR; Fox Factory subsidiary).
- **VanDOit:** MOOV, LIV, DO (Transit, modular).
- **Rossmonster:** Skye (Sprinter); Loft and Havn are B+.
- **Dave & Matt Vans:** LV3, LV5, LV7 (ProMaster, direct-to-consumer value).
- **Glampervan:** MUV 136 (ProMaster upfit).
- **TOURIG:** bespoke Sprinter builds, no production line.
- **Titan Vans:** Classic / Metis / Ultra families (Sprinter + Transit, 10 variants).
- **Field Van:** SHYFT, Summit S1.
- **Advanced RV:** ultra-custom one-offs, from ~$550k, no fixed models.
- **ModVans:** CV1 / MH1 families (Transit pop-tops).
- **Vanworks:** Switchback line (Sprinter).
- **Benchmark Vehicles:** fully custom Sprinter overland.
- Not builders: Van City RV (dealer), RoamBuilt (accessories). Excluded.

- Unverified items remain in the research notes (.research/) and enter the ledger only after manufacturer confirmation.

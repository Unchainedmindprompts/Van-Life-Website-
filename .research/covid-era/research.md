# Research: COVID-era used Class B article (researched Oct 14, 2026)

## Verdict in one line
The COVID quality collapse is documented for high-volume towables; for Class B the hard evidence is different in kind: big recalls trace to SUPPLIER components, no defect-rate-by-model-year dataset exists, Class B production peaked in 2022 (a year after towables), and no source shows dealers discounting 2021-22 units for quality.

## Production (RVIA, DATA)
- All RVs: 2019 406,070 | 2020 430,412 | 2021 600,240 (record) | 2022 493,268 | 2023 313,174
- Type B van campers: 2019 4,248 | 2020 7,222 (+70%) | 2021 13,827 (+91.5%) | 2022 17,026 (PEAK, +23.1%) | 2023 11,932
- Elkhart unemployment 1% late 2021 (NYT); wages +35% vs early 2020; Winnebago worker 16 -> 36 RVs/day (IndyStar, origin of "COVID trailers"); EDC Elkhart: "In 2022, manufacturers overproduced."
- Worst labor-shortage window per owner reporting: H2 2021 - H1 2022.

## Class B recalls (NHTSA, DATA) - mostly supplier defects
- Thor 22V-300: 3,374 units incl. 2022 Scope/Tellaro/Sanctuary/Sequence/Tranquility/Rize; cracked LP quick-disconnect fittings; cause = Enerco brass porosity/contamination. Supplier.
- Thor 21V-755: 2022 Scope/Rize LP line too short, bad bend (installation defect).
- Thor 21V-396: 2022 Rize/Scope/Sequence/Tellaro Flex-190 solar junction box short.
- Thor awning recall: 2022-2023 Rize/Scope awning extends in transit.
- Winnebago 23V-307: 6,536 vans MY2020-2023 (Travato/Solis/Ekko); Cummins Onan QG 2800i generator fuel hose clamp force. Supplier (Cummins spec). Window runs to Apr 2023 builds.
- Winnebago 22V-863: 4,521 Revels 2021-2023; Carefree awning gearbox housing. Supplier.
- Winnebago Suburban cooktop: 9,720 units (Solis/Travato/Ekko); burner tubes fracture. Supplier. Coachmen same cooktop: only 12 units.
- Winnebago 21V-793: 2022 Travato/Boldt wire harness built incorrectly (workmanship).
- Counter-example: Winnebago Era 170X seat-belt weld nut, MY2012-2021 (mostly pre-COVID).
- TMC baseline: ~10 recall campaigns/year typical (secondary compilation).

## Towables contrast (DATA)
- Hunterbrook/NHTSA: "frame" complaints spike = Grand Design MY2020-2022 fifth wheels; NHTSA investigation PE24029 (frame flex, Lippert frames). No equivalent for any Class B model.
- JD Power 2023 YE: standard hitch -4.0%, fifth wheels -2.5%, Class A -2.5%, Class C +1.3%; Class B not broken out. Correction hit towables hardest.
- NRVIA inspector Duane (RVtravel): recommended used Grand Design years stop at 2020 "because COVID-era effects" - a FIFTH WHEEL judgment, do not quote as Class B verdict.
- RVtravel poll (>1,200 owners, not era-specific): 76% rate their RV well/very well built; ~4% poorly built/junk.

## Market (DATA + EXPERT)
- Lender commentary (Octane VP, Powersports Finance Summit 2026): pandemic buyers paid full price, negative equity overhang - first owner absorbed peak price + correction; favors the 2026 used buyer.
- NOT FOUND: any dealer/valuation source discounting or refusing 2021-22 units for quality reasons.

## Counter-case / buying protocol (the article's actionable core)
- Recalls are free remedies, VIN-checkable at nhtsa.gov; by 2026 mostly completed; biggest Class B recalls are one-part supplier swaps.
- First owner absorbed the warranty punch list (consistent forum advice across eras).
- NRVIA inspection scope: water intrusion/prior damage, roof + sealant, LP leak test, detector dates, tire DOT age, electrical, structure, solar. Inspection = a few hundred dollars (RV Trader guide). No era-specific checklist exists; era advice = verify recall completion + weight water/sealant checks.
- Service conditions era color (ANECDOTE, iRV2 Dec 2021): independent shops $150/hr vs $135 pre-COVID, 4-6 wk backlogs vs 2-3.

## Window definition (defensible)
Built ~June 2020 - Dec 2022 (mostly MY2021-MY2023); highest-risk sub-window mid-2021 to mid-2022. Note Class B volume peaked 2022; generator recall reaches Apr 2023 builds - no clean cutoff.

## NOT substantiated (do not print)
" Toxic Years" as an industry term (YouTube only; sourced nickname is "COVID trailers"); Elkhart 0.9% unemployment (sourced: 1%); systematic dealer avoidance/discounting for quality; any "% worse" defect figure (dataset does not exist publicly); Consumer Reports RV reliability data (CR has no RV coverage); lemon-law era patterns; a Class B-specific quality collapse.
